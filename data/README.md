# Card data

`cards.yml` contains detailed website records. `discovery.yml` contains the wider
card-by-card research queue. Both are shown on the website; discovery records use
`Researching` instead of pretending that an unchecked fee or benefit is known.
Amounts are in Indian rupees and exclude GST unless a record says otherwise.

Each record links to a primary issuer source. `verification: verified` means the
fee schedule was checked against an official issuer page or key-fact statement;
`partial` means at least one material benefit still needs a second pass.

`issuers.yml` is the issuer ledger. `coverage: discovery` means the issuer's cards
have not yet passed the project's detailed-data bar. A `discovery.yml` entry is a
candidate with an official verification target; it does not by itself assert that
the card is currently issued or that new applications are open.

Never use referral or affiliate pages as the sole source for a fee, waiver,
reward rate, eligibility rule, or benefit.
